Books the browser payload can include

Generated from the payload manifest when the site is built, so this is what is present, not what is intended. A book is includable when both its .cert (ACL2's certificate) and its wasm .fasc (compiled from ACL2's own expansion file) are shipped.

Type any line below at the prompt in ACL2 in the browser. :dir :system resolves to the payload's books/ tree. include-book may be used as often as you like in one session; it is certification, not inclusion, that is limited to one book per ACL2 session.

Stack. Including a book loads its compiled module, and a Chrome page has about 1 MB of engine stack for that. The tutorial books and the small system/ and tools/ books fit. Two do not, measured: projects/apply/base needs ~1.05 MB and projects/apply/top ~1.09 MB, so in Chrome they are refused with a message; a Firefox page has about three times Chrome's stack and loads them (top takes 4.4 s there). This is a limit of the engine's recursion while loading a module, not of the book's own events.

Not every book in ACL2's community books/ tree is here: each one has to be certified and compiled ahead of time, and the page preloads the whole list into memory. The list is wasm/web/books.txt in the port; adding a book is wasm/certify-books.sh, wasm/aot-book.sh, one line in that file, and a rebuild of the payload. Thinking in terms of a book set per use case is the right shape: this payload is one such set.
